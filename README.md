#  Evidence Vault – Digital Evidence Management System

##  About the Project
Evidence Vault is a Digital Evidence Management System designed to organize and manage digital evidence and investigation-related records in a structured manner.
The system aims to simplify evidence management, maintain proper case records, and support efficient digital investigation workflows.

##  Objectives
- To maintain digital evidence records systematically.
- To manage investigation cases efficiently.
- To organize evidence and case-related information.
- To improve the accessibility and management of digital records.
- To maintain relationships between cases and their associated evidence.

##  Key Features
- **Case Management:** Create and manage investigation cases.
- **Evidence Management:** Maintain digital evidence records.
- **Investigation Records:** Organize investigation-related information.
- **Database Management:** Store and manage structured records.
- **Data Organization:** Maintain relationships between cases and evidence.

- Database Management System
